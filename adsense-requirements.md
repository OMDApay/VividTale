# Google AdSense implementation notes

Google’s official eligibility guidance says a site should have original, high-quality content that attracts an audience, comply with AdSense Program Policies, and be owned and editable by the publisher; applicants must be at least 18 or use an adult guardian account. Source: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en

Google’s required-content guidance says the privacy policy should disclose that third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on visits to this and other sites; it should explain Google’s advertising-cookie use, link to Ads Settings or an equivalent opt-out resource, and identify other ad networks/vendors when applicable. Source: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1348695?hl=en

Implementation decision: add visible About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Advertising pages/sections; clearly label the reserved ad area; keep ad code out until the publisher has an approved AdSense account and the final publisher IDs are available; avoid claiming guaranteed approval because Google reviews each site and policy details can change.
